Artificial intelligence (AI) has dominated conversations among health plans and providers, given its potential to transform the industry. From predictive models for disease onset to chatbots that answer member questions, the applications seem endless. Yet, not all solutions deliver meaningful results to health plans, particularly when it comes to reducing total cost of care and improving member engagement.
